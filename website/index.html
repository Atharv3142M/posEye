01 / THE IDEALOCAL-FIRST · OPEN SOURCE

One flight.
A new point of view.

posEye is an experimental desktop workflow that transforms drone video and flight metadata into georeferenced 3D outputs—while keeping processing on your machine.

Not another cloud upload. A transparent pipeline you can inspect, run, and build upon.
