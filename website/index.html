01 / THE SYSTEMFROM FLIGHT TO SPATIAL DATA

One flight.
A spatial record.

posEye is an open-source desktop project exploring how a single continuous drone flight can become a georeferenced, inspectable 3D reconstruction.

Video is processed alongside GPS and flight metadata. The aim is to keep the pipeline understandable, local-first, and grounded in measurable outputs.
