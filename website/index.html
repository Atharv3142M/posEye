[ 00 — THE IDEA ]

A drone flight is more than a recording.
It is a dataset waiting to be understood.

posEye is an experimental, local-first application for converting a single-pass UAV video and its flight metadata into a navigable 3D reconstruction.

[ 03 — THE OUTPUT ]

Not just a map.
A place you can inspect.

When reconstruction succeeds, the result can be viewed as geometry—not merely a flat image. posEye's direction is to make that output easier to inspect, understand and carry into other tools.
