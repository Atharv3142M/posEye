01 / THE WORKFLOW SINGLE-PASS VIDEO TO GEOREFERENCED ASSETS

From raw aerial video to an inspectable spatial workspace.

Mapping software typically forces pilots to capture manual grids of still photos and push gigabytes to remote servers. posEye starts directly from standard drone video and `.srt` telemetry logs.

The desktop orchestrator handles frame selection, sharpness filtering, GPS EXIF tagging, local photogrammetry via the posEye Construction Engine, and origin-centered WebGL inspection in one continuous run.
