01 · RECONSTRUCTION

Shape vs Metric Scale

Structure-from-Motion (SfM) inherently recovers 3D shape up to an arbitrary scale factor. Drone GPS telemetry anchors that scale into physical meters, but uncalibrated altitude sensors can drift without ground control.

02 · TELEMETRY

Consumer GPS vs RTK/PPK

Consumer drone barometric and GNSS telemetry provides ±3m horizontal and ±5m vertical accuracy. RTK (Real-Time Kinematic) base stations achieve sub-2cm positioning by resolving carrier phase ambiguities.

03 · VERIFICATION

GCPs & Independent Checkpoints

True survey verification requires Ground Control Points (GCPs) and independent checkpoints measured with high-precision total stations or GNSS rovers to compute Root Mean Square Error (RMSE).
