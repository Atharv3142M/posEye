01 / validating STAGE 01–02 // FFMPEG INGEST
STATE: validatingPHASE 01

Media & Telemetry Probe

Verifies video container integrity, codec streams, resolution, framerate, duration, and SRT telemetry bounds before allocating job storage.

STATE: extractingPHASE 02

Static FFmpeg Frame Sampling

Managed subprocess extraction pulls candidate frames at configurable rates using the bundled offline FFmpeg binary, with instant clean teardown on cancel.

STATE: filteringPHASE 03

Laplacian Blur & dHash Deduplication

Drops motion-blurred frames via OpenCV Laplacian variance and eliminates hover redundancy with 64-bit perceptual hashing (~70–80% overlap target).

STATE: geotaggingPHASE 04

WGS 84 EXIF & Timestamp Provenance

Matches frames to SRT cues within a strict 1.5s window, writes EPSG:4326 DMS coordinates, preserves AMSL altitude (`GPSAltitudeRef`), and stamps true capture times.

STATE: maskingPHASE 05

Dynamic Obstruction Masking

Optional YOLOv8n segmentation identifies transient vehicles, personnel, and animals, generating per-frame masks so moving objects never ghost the static mesh.

STATE: dispatchingPHASE 06

Loopback Engine Dispatch

Packages geotagged frames and optional masks into an authenticated local payload dispatched strictly over 127.0.0.1 to the posEye Construction Engine.

STATE: reconstructingPHASE 07

posEye Construction Engine

Executes sparse bundle adjustment, dense multi-view point generation, Poisson surface meshing, texture baking, and orthomosaic synthesis—with verified CUDA GPU acceleration or CPU fallback.

STATE: postprocessingPHASE 08

Zip-Slip Hardened Extraction

Unpacks output archives through canonical path confinement, blocking directory traversal, symlinks, encrypted bombs, and oversized payloads.

STATE: convertingPHASE 09

Draco GLB & Origin Centering

Transforms surface meshes into Draco-compressed binary glTF, centers bounding boxes to (0, 0, 0) for jitter-free WebGL, and validates headers and vertex buffers.

STATE: readyPHASE 10

Immutable Completion & Export

Persists job metadata and accuracy provenance to job.json, streaming the verified model into the 3D viewport alongside .LAZ, .TIF, and .PDF exports.

STATE: cancellation_requested → cancelledPHASE 11

Graceful Subprocess Cancellation

Operators can abort active runs safely at any phase; worker trees terminate cleanly, partial scratch artifacts are purged, and terminal states remain immutable.
